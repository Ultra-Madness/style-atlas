# Swatch Aperture

Color becomes a compact index: six carefully balanced planes turn around one
quiet center. The symbol suggests looking, comparing and collecting without
depending on a letter or a miniature picture.

A deep ink field gives the mark its silhouette. Warm cream and olive connect
to the gallery, while coral, amber and cool blue-green make it easy to locate
among other phone icons. Each boundary is deliberately spaced and crisp.

The restrained geometry carries the detail. Broad facets and a generous central
opening retain their rhythm when reduced to favicon size. Meticulous alignment
and consistent dark seams provide the finish rather than decorative effects.

All artwork stays comfortably inside the platform's maskable safe circle.
Phone operating systems supply the outer shape; the source stays opaque and
square. The same visual identity scales from the small header mark to a large
home-screen icon with no text inside the artwork.
